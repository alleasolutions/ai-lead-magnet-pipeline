Frequently asked questions

Does this apply if I'm already using a cold email tool?

Yes — most cold email platforms handle sending, but deliverability fundamentals (domain setup, warm-up, authentication) are on you regardless of the tool.

How long does mailbox warm-up actually take?

Plan for 2-3 weeks of gradual ramp-up before sending at full volume — rushing this is the most common cause of poor inbox placement.

Do I need multiple mailboxes if I only send a little?

If you're staying under 50 emails/day from one mailbox, a single well-warmed inbox can work — rotation matters most once you scale past that.

Is this checklist specific to one email provider?

No — the fundamentals (domain isolation, authentication, warm-up, list hygiene) apply whether you're sending through Gmail, Outlook, or a dedicated sending platform.
